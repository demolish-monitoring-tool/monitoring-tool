<p class="text-align-justify MsoNormal"><span>DeMoLiSH takes active measures to ensure that individuals or organizations involved in any stage of this project will not be harmed. Both consortium members have established Research Ethics Committees [1,2], the guidelines of which will be respected. Personal data of the project’s website users will be protected according to the GÉANT Data Protection Code of Conduct for Service Providers. The data collection process as well as the databases’ distribution will be in compliance with the data providers (news portals, Twitter API) terms of use and services and with the GDPR regulation. Given that Tweets contain identifying information, minimally the user ID, and possibly location or other identifying content, their processing needs to follow the GDPR [3]. Taking into consideration issues surrounding the ethics of using Twitter data for research purposes, in the context of DeMoLiSH, only the content of the tweet text will be analyzed, and the anonymity of tweet authors will be protected ensuring that users will not be identifiable. Furthermore, the Twitter data will not be used to harvest additional information from other sources; no personal or sensitive data will be included in the databases; only the VA analysis results, and the timestamp of the Tweets will be recorded and distributed. Similarly, the Event database will not include any sensitive data.
